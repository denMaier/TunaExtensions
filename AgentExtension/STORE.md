Type a question or a task, choose **Ask Agent**, and Tuna hands it to the agent you already use on the command line: Claude Code, Codex, or Antigravity. You can also start with the command (**Ask Agent**, then **Ask…**) or select files and choose **Ask Agent About…**.

The agent works in the background. If it finished a task and there's nothing to read, a small **Agent: Done!** banner appears. Answers, and anything that went wrong, come back into Tuna as text you can copy, paste, or add to the Shelf.

The agent runs as you, in your home folder, with its own unattended safety mode. Requests go to the agent's provider under your account and may cost usage. Needs the chosen CLI installed and signed in.
